# About Nu Labs: Human behind the code

Status: implemented with the generated transparent asset. Final page QA is recorded separately in `docs/about-qa.md`.

## Brief

Help prospective clients recognise a youthful South African development house with a shared love of software and design through the tension between a vintage machine and a distinctly human voice.

The supplied facts are: Mikey Nu (Pty) Ltd; founded in 2023; an online business with remote staff; based in Fourways, South Africa; a youthful, creative team with common goals. No staff identities, street address, certifications, ownership percentages or client results are inferred.

## Three routes considered

1. **Human behind the code.** An editorial light poster with a floating computer, a dark binary chapter and a horizontal sequence about curiosity, collaboration and craft. The people remain the subject. Main risk: the computer can collide with the large type. Selected because it connects the user's object request to the team story and provides a clear change in rhythm.
2. **Remote terminal.** A black terminal field with a central computer and company facts arranged as connected nodes. Strong atmosphere, but risks presenting a software product instead of the people and makes the page too dark.
3. **The 2023 archive.** A huge founding year, a computer catalogue composition and a chronological narrative. Useful factual anchor, but a short company history does not support an elaborate timeline without invented milestones.

Comparable roughs: `art/about/roughs.html`. They use the same facts and a clearly labelled vector blockout. The blockout is a composition aid, not the requested generated asset.

## Composition and copy

- Opening: oversized `Human / behind the / code.` with the computer balanced in the right-hand negative space. The small introduction explains the actual business. No pill, badge cluster or conventional button stack.
- Black chapter: `A dev house. A different mindset.` Binary columns sit at the edges, outside the main reading field. The original Nu Labs logo stays unchanged.
- Thinking chapter: three large horizontal panels on spacious screens. The movement explains a progression from curiosity to collaboration to useful work. Native vertical cards on phones, short viewports, reduced-motion and no-JavaScript sessions.
- Remote chapter: `Different desks. One direction.` Fourways appears as a typographic point of connection, not a fabricated map or headquarters photo. The legal name, founding year and remote working model are presented plainly.
- Closing: `Got a good “What if?”` leads to the existing contact route without implying that the local enquiry tool sends messages.

The `/studio/` URL is retained; visible navigation and breadcrumbs become About. Metadata stays unique. Organization schema uses the supplied legal name, founding year and a place name, with no invented postal address.

## Visual system

Existing Instrument Sans, normal variable weights 400–700, is retained. Display type spans roughly 58–280px on desktop and fluid sizes on phones. Body text is 17–28px according to role. UI monospace is restricted to dates and small navigation-like details. Essential text is live HTML.

Paper `#f7f7f2`, ink `#111111`, white `#fdfdfa`, secondary text `#595959` on paper and `#c6c6c6` on black. Binary decoration is low contrast by design and carries no essential information. No neon green, glow or rapid glitch effects.

## Motion thesis

Native scroll is the only scroll owner. The computer moves up by at most 38px and rotates by at most five additional degrees as the opening leaves. Binary columns drift only with scrolling. There are no autoplay loops. The finite horizontal chapter measures real content overflow, reverses with scrolling and releases after the last panel. Reading copy remains fully present in the DOM.

Spatial movement is disabled when reduced motion is requested. The horizontal chapter also becomes ordinary flow below 1101px width or 800px height. No framework, animation library or runtime package is added.
